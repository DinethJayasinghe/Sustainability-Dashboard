ESG Data Governance Model & Data Dictionary

Controlled measurement standards, calculation formulas, and data quality boundary notes (Sections 26 & 27)

No Unsupported Targets

Do not invent future numerical milestones where baselines are not yet scientifically established. Mark as TBE.

Boundary Consistency

Local supplier spending series experienced boundary shift after FY23. Do not treat full series as identical without disclosure.

Input vs Outcome Pairing

Community investment (Rs. Mn) must always be paired with outcome impact verification, not standalone spending.

Single Executive Accountability

Every operational KPI must have one named primary business owner accountable for investigation & corrective action.

5-Year Strategic Implementation Roadmap (FY 2026/27 – FY 2030/31)

Structured phased execution plan for EPP Board of Directors (Sections 28–38)

YEAR 1 • FY 2026/27

Standardise & Establish Baselines

Foundation Phase
  • Confirm formal definitions for all 20 Board KPIs and document ESG data governance rules.
  • Maintain Madakumbura elderly home as operational SDG 3 milestone.
  • Implement quarterly region-wise PPE & safety-wear awareness sessions.
  • Establish missing baselines for Scope 3, renewable ratio, freshwater intensity, waste diversion, senior female %, voluntary turnover, Board reviews, ethics SLAs, and community outcomes.
  • Create baseline measures for SDG 4 school targets and SDG 12 land/bio-fertiliser targets.
YEAR 2 • FY 2027/28

Deliver 2028 Milestones

Execution Phase
  • Achieve low-country zero-hunger milestone by 2028 (Target T01).
  • Commence construction/launch of 2nd elderly home at Dunsinane (Target T04).
  • Scale vertical/hydroponic farming and bio-fertiliser programmes with initial yield tracking.
  • Drive injury-reduction pathway toward fewer than 30 incidents.
  • Introduce outcome reporting for Grade 1–5 school attendance and sanitary facility upgrades.
YEAR 3 • FY 2028/29

Embed Outcome Measurement

Optimization Phase
  • Use at least two full years of standardized baseline data to set evidence-based numerical targets.
  • Integrate supplier ESG compliance percentages & corrective action SLAs into procurement contracts.
  • Strengthen community outcome measurement beyond expenditure & headcounts.
  • Review GHG, energy and water trends for multi-year structural efficiency gains.
YEAR 4 • FY 2029/30

Extend & Integrate

Integration Phase
  • Extend zero-hunger programme toward up-country operations & group-wide reach.
  • Integrate Board sustainability review completion & ethics case SLA closure into core governance.
  • Link diversified/value-added revenue & innovation measures to executive performance incentives.
  • Maintain synchronized KPI definitions across annual report, Board packs and Power BI.
YEAR 5 • FY 2030/31

Sustain & Reset Target Cycle

Transformation Phase
  • Maintain 2030 group-wide zero-hunger outcome sustainably.
  • Maintain recordable injuries below 30 incidents & zero fatalities/high-consequence injuries.
  • Demonstrate measurable progress in water, energy, waste diversion, supplier ESG & community.
  • Review 20-KPI framework and approve next 5-year cycle (2031–2036) based on proven evidence.
